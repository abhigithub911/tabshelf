# Tabshelf Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 17, 2026_

Tabshelf is a browser extension that replaces the new tab page with a board for saving tabs, links, notes and to-dos.

## What Tabshelf stores
Tabshelf stores what you save (site titles and addresses, notes, to-dos, images you save, reminders and your settings)
in your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your own device. It is never sent to the developer
or to any server operated by the developer. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

## How Tabshelf uses browser permissions
- **Tabs and tab groups:** to list the tabs in your current window in the sidebar, save them into groups, switch to them, and open saved sites. Tab information is used only on your device.
- **Storage:** to keep your board on your device.
- **Context menus:** to add "Save to Tabshelf" options to the right-click menu.
- **Notifications and alarms:** to show the reminders you set.
- **Top sites:** to optionally create a "Most visited" group from Chrome's own list.
- **Bookmarks (optional, asked only when you use it):** to import a bookmarks folder you choose.
- **Search:** to send a search to your default search engine when you choose "Search the web" in Tabshelf's search.
- **Favicon:** to show site icons from Chrome's local icon cache.

## Third parties
To show icons for sites you haven't visited, Tabshelf can request icons from Google's public favicon service
(`www.google.com/s2/favicons`), which receives the site's domain name (not the full address, and nothing else you saved).
You can turn this off in *Settings → Fetch missing site icons*. Tabshelf does not sell or transfer your data to anyone.

## Your control
You can export all your data at any time (*Settings → Export backup*), and you can delete it all with
*Settings → Reset Tabshelf* or by removing the extension.
